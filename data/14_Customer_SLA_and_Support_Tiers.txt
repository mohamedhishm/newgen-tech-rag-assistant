NEWGEN TECH
CUSTOMER SLA AND SUPPORT TIERS
Document ID: NGT-CS-014 | Owner: Customer Support

1. SUPPORT TIERS
Standard: business-hours support, named tenant administrators, public status updates.
Pro: extended support, priority queue, quarterly service review.
Enterprise: 24x7 SEV-1/SEV-2 coverage, named escalation contacts, service review, and contracted architecture support.

2. INITIAL RESPONSE TARGETS
Severity | Standard | Pro | Enterprise
SEV-1 critical outage/security exposure | 2 business hours | 30 minutes, 24x7 | 15 minutes, 24x7
SEV-2 major degradation | 4 business hours | 2 hours | 30 minutes, 24x7
SEV-3 limited impact/workaround exists | 1 business day | 8 business hours | 4 business hours
SEV-4 question/request | 2 business days | 1 business day | 1 business day

3. SEVERITY DEFINITIONS
SEV-1: broad production unavailability, confirmed cross-tenant exposure, active critical compromise, or data-loss condition.
SEV-2: major feature unavailable, severe degradation, or multiple users blocked without reasonable workaround.
SEV-3: limited feature impairment or isolated user impact with workaround.
SEV-4: how-to, configuration, documentation, or enhancement request.

4. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS
Provide product, tenant, environment, timestamps, correlation IDs, impact, troubleshooting already performed, and a reachable technical contact. Do not send credentials or full customer datasets by email.

5. ESCALATION MATRIX
SEV-1: Support Duty Manager -> Incident Commander -> Product Engineering Lead -> Information Security Officer when security is involved -> CTO as required.
SEV-2: Support Lead -> Engineering service owner -> Product owner.
Commercial or service-credit question: Customer Success -> Commercial Operations -> Legal/Finance.

6. STATUS UPDATES
SEV-1 updates at least every 30 minutes until stable. SEV-2 updates at least every 2 hours during active work. Lower-severity updates follow the agreed case plan.

7. AVAILABILITY
Availability commitments and service-credit terms are defined in the signed order or master agreement. Maintenance announced under the release policy, customer-caused failure, unsupported configuration, and force majeure may be excluded as stated in contract.

8. ESCALATION CONTACT
Use the Support Portal for cases. For an open Enterprise SEV-1 case, use the published 24x7 escalation number and quote the case ID.
