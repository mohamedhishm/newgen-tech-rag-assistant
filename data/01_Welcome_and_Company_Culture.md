**Classification:** Internal

## 1. Welcome
NewGen Tech was founded in 2021 to make enterprise cloud operations, analytics, and digital security easier to manage without sacrificing resilience or control. Approximately 180 employees work from the Cairo and Alexandria hubs and approved remote locations.

## 2. Mission
Help organizations operate secure, intelligent, and resilient digital infrastructure without unnecessary complexity.

## 3. Products
- **NovaCloud:** cloud infrastructure inventory, policy, cost, capacity, and operational-health management.
- **PulseAI:** enterprise predictive analytics and automated reporting.
- **ShieldGate:** zero-trust identity and API security gateway.

## 4. Values
### Earn Trust Through Clarity
Document decisions, distinguish facts from assumptions, and raise risk early.
### Own the Outcome
Coordinate dependencies, measure results, and complete the handoff.
### Build Securely by Default
Apply least privilege, approved systems, and early security review.
### Learn, Test, Improve
Use customer evidence, operational metrics, and blameless reviews.
### Respect the Person, Challenge the Work
Debate ideas directly without hostility, exclusion, or humiliation.
### Design for the Customer's Reality
Account for cost, regulation, staffing, migration, and legacy systems.

## 5. Operating Model
NewGen Tech is hybrid and remote-first. Information required to work must be available in approved systems rather than informal office conversations. Slack supports discussion, while Jira, Confluence, repositories, and controlled record systems remain the sources of truth.

Core collaboration hours and coworking support are defined in `02_Work_Hours_and_Hybrid_Policy.pdf`.

## 8. Employee Responsibilities
Employees must protect company and customer information, keep records accurate, complete required training, report security or conduct concerns, use resources responsibly, and ask when instructions are unclear.

## 9. Decision Rights
People policy is owned by the VP of People & HR; technology standards by the CTO; product priorities by the Head of Product; and security exceptions by the Information Security Officer. Employees may not make financial, contractual, public, or hiring commitments without authority.
