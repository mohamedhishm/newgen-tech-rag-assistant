HR and Payroll Master FAQ

Document ID: NGT-FAQ-023 | NewGen Tech | Internal | Effective 1 January 2026

When is salary paid?

Salary is paid on the 25th calendar day. If it falls on a weekend or recognized banking holiday, payment is initiated on the preceding business day.

Where can I see tax and social-insurance deductions?

Open the monthly payslip. It separates gross pay, taxable items, statutory deductions, benefit deductions, and net pay.

How do I report a payroll error?

Submit a Payroll Support request within 30 calendar days and attach the payslip month and the specific line in question.

Can I change my bank account?

Yes. Submit verified details by the 10th calendar day for the best chance of affecting that month. Never send full banking documents in a public Slack channel.

How many annual-leave days do I receive?

Regular full-time employees receive 21 working days per calendar year, accrued monthly.

How much leave carries over?

Up to five annual-leave days carry over and must normally be used by 31 March.

How do I request leave?

Use the HR system, select dates and leave type, add a handover note if needed, and wait for manager approval before making non-refundable plans.

How many sick days are available?

Regular full-time employees receive 10 paid working days per calendar year. A certificate may be requested after more than two consecutive working days.

What parental leave is available?

The policy provides 120 calendar days paid maternity leave, 15 working days paid paternity or partner leave, and defined adoption leave, subject to greater legal rights.

Can I add insurance dependents?

Eligible spouses or partners and dependent children may be enrolled under insurer rules. Submit qualifying-event documentation within 30 calendar days.

Which medical tier applies to me?

Tier A normally applies to L1-L2, Tier B to L3-L4, and Tier C to L5-L6. The current insurer certificate controls actual limits.

What is the learning budget?

Regular employees may use up to USD 1,200 or approved local equivalent each calendar year for role-relevant learning and conferences.

Is the coworking stipend taxable or paid automatically?

It is an expense reimbursement, not automatic cash. Eligible receipts are capped at EGP 1,500 per month; tax treatment follows payroll and local rules.

Can I take a sabbatical?

Employees with three years of continuous service may request an unpaid four-to-twelve-week sabbatical at least 90 days in advance.

What happens to benefits when I leave?

People & HR confirms the medical-insurance end date, final payroll, leave treatment, and any continuation option during offboarding.
