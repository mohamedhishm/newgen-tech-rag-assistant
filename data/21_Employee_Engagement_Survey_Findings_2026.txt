NEWGEN TECH
EMPLOYEE ENGAGEMENT SURVEY FINDINGS 2026
Document ID: NGT-RPT-021 | Owner: People & HR

1. PARTICIPATION
Survey open: 6-20 September 2026. Responses: 151 of approximately 180 employees. Response rate: 84%. Results are reported in groups of at least five to protect confidentiality.

2. OVERALL SCORES
Overall engagement: 78/100.
Pride in NewGen Tech: 84.
Manager support: 81.
Role clarity: 76.
Career growth: 69.
Workload sustainability: 67.
Hybrid-work effectiveness: 82.
Confidence in leadership communication: 74.

3. STRONGEST THEMES
Employees value remote-first flexibility, approachable managers, product mission, and cross-team willingness to help during incidents. Respondents especially noted improved engineering documentation and the predictability of 11 AM-3 PM collaboration hours.

4. IMPROVEMENT THEMES
Career expectations are not equally clear across business functions. Some teams experience meeting concentration during core hours. Cairo-hub capacity is tight on planning days. Engineering and Customer Success report recurring after-hours workload. Employees want clearer compensation timing and more visibility into roadmap trade-offs.

5. HYBRID-WORK ACTIONS
- Publish team operating agreements by 31 January 2027.
- Pilot two meeting-light mornings per week in Product and Engineering.
- Add 24 reservable Cairo desks and improve focus-room booking by Q1 2027.
- Retain the EGP 1,500 monthly coworking stipend.
- Require remote participation for cross-site company meetings.

6. CAREER AND MANAGER ACTIONS
People & HR will publish business-function examples for L1-L6, train managers on review evidence, and introduce quarterly development conversations. Each department will identify roles with weak succession coverage.

7. WORKLOAD ACTIONS
Engineering Operations will reduce repeated PagerDuty alerts by 25% in Q4. Customer Success will add regional backup ownership for Enterprise accounts. Managers will review employees with sustained after-hours work during monthly capacity planning.

8. ACCOUNTABILITY
Sarah El-Masry owns the company action plan. Department leaders report progress monthly. A pulse survey in March 2027 will repeat career clarity, workload, leadership communication, and hybrid-effectiveness questions.

9. LIMITATIONS
Results reflect participant perceptions at the survey date. Small teams were combined, free-text was redacted, and scores should not be interpreted as legal or clinical findings.
