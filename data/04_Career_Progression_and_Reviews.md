# Career Progression and Performance Reviews

**Policy code:** NGT-HR-004  
**Owner:** People & HR  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Framework
NewGen Tech uses six levels across engineering, product, and business functions. Promotion reflects sustained next-level scope, not tenure alone.

| Level | Designation | Typical scope |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate | Defined tasks with regular guidance |
| L2 | Professional | Independent ownership of standard work |
| L3 | Senior | Complex work and team-level influence |
| L4 | Lead / Staff | Cross-team initiatives and standards |
| L5 | Principal / Director | Product or department-wide strategy |
| L6 | Distinguished / Executive | Company-wide direction and accountability |

## 2. Shared Dimensions
Results and impact; role expertise; ownership and execution; collaboration and communication; values, security, and conduct.

## 3. Engineering Ladder
- **L1 Associate Engineer:** guided changes, tests, documentation, and secure tool use.
- **L2 Engineer:** independent features, maintainable code, troubleshooting, reviews, and on-call participation.
- **L3 Senior Engineer:** complex services, architecture trade-offs, mentoring, incident leadership, and risk reduction.
- **L4 Staff Engineer / Engineering Lead:** multi-team architecture, standards, migrations, cost and reliability programs.
- **L5 Principal Engineer / Engineering Director:** product-wide technical strategy, organizational capability, and material risk decisions.
- **L6 Distinguished Engineer / Technology Executive:** company-wide portfolio direction across NovaCloud, PulseAI, and ShieldGate.

## 4. Business and Product Ladder
- **L1 Associate:** accurate work using established procedures.
- **L2 Specialist / Manager:** independent ownership of a process, customer set, campaign, or product area.
- **L3 Senior Specialist / Senior Manager:** ambiguous work, complex stakeholders, and team-level impact.
- **L4 Lead / Head:** major function, segment, product area, or cross-company program.
- **L5 Director / Principal:** multi-year strategy and substantial business outcomes.
- **L6 Vice President / Executive:** company-wide accountability and executive governance.

## 5. Review Cycles
| Cycle | Period | Review | Effective changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-year | January-June | June-July | Normally August payroll |
| Year-end | July-December with full-year context | December-January | Normally February payroll |

## 6. Process
Goal confirmation, regular one-to-ones, employee self-review, manager assessment, calibration, review conversation, and HR-system acknowledgment. Employees should not first learn of a known material concern during the formal review.

## 7. Ratings
5 Exceptional Impact; 4 Exceeds Expectations; 3 Meets Expectations; 2 Partially Meets Expectations; 1 Does Not Meet Expectations. A 3 represents successful performance.

## 8. Promotion Criteria
Scope, measurable impact, consistency, conduct, readiness, and organizational need. A common evidence period is at least six months. L5 and L6 changes require Executive Leadership Team review.

## 9. Review Questions
Factual concerns may be submitted to People & HR within 10 business days. HR reviews process, omitted evidence, consistency, and possible bias or retaliation.

## 10. Performance Improvement
Formal plans normally last 30-90 calendar days and define required improvement, evidence, support, check-ins, and potential outcomes. People & HR reviews the plan before issue.

## 11. Development
Development may include stretch work, mentoring, rotations, customer exposure, certification, and conference participation using the annual USD 1,200 budget in `07_Compensation_Benefits_and_Health_Insurance.pdf`.
