# Parental, Compassionate, and Sabbatical Leave

**Policy code:** NGT-HR-006  
**Document owner:** People & HR  
**Approved by:** Sarah El-Masry, VP of People & HR  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Version:** 1.4  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Applies to:** Regular employees of NewGen Tech

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains paid and unpaid leave available for childbirth, adoption, bereavement, urgent family circumstances, and approved sabbaticals. It is designed for NewGen Tech's hybrid, remote-first workforce while preserving team coverage, employee privacy, and all greater rights provided by law or an employment agreement.

## 2. Governing Principles

- Statutory or contractual rights that are more favorable than this policy prevail.
- Leave decisions must not discriminate based on gender, marital status, family status, pregnancy, disability, religion, or another protected characteristic.
- Employees should disclose only the information needed to administer leave.
- Managers must not request medical diagnoses or detailed family records.
- People & HR maintains official leave records and determines the applicable category.
- Performance ratings, promotion decisions, and work allocation must not penalize an employee for taking approved protected leave.

## 3. Maternity Leave

Eligible employees giving birth receive **120 calendar days of paid maternity leave** per birth event.

### 3.1 Timing

- Leave may begin up to 30 calendar days before the expected delivery date.
- The employee may request a different start date based on medical advice and applicable law.
- Any medically required absence before the maternity-leave start date is recorded under the appropriate sick or statutory leave category.
- Where hospitalization or medical complications create additional statutory rights, People & HR will apply the more favorable entitlement.

### 3.2 Request Process

The employee should contact People & HR at least eight weeks before the expected start date when reasonably possible. The request should include:

1. Expected delivery date.
2. Proposed leave start date.
3. Expected return date.
4. Medical confirmation of the expected delivery date, submitted confidentially to People & HR.
5. Any proposed annual leave immediately before or after maternity leave.

Late notice will be accepted when circumstances could not reasonably have been predicted.

### 3.3 Pay and Benefits

- Base salary continues during the 120-day paid period.
- Medical insurance continues under the employee's existing enrollment.
- Annual leave continues to accrue unless a local mandatory rule requires different treatment.
- Variable compensation is handled under the relevant plan terms and must not be reduced solely because protected leave was taken.
- Company equipment remains assigned unless IT or Information Security identifies a specific operational need.

## 4. Paternity and Partner Leave

An eligible employee whose spouse or recognized partner gives birth receives **15 working days of paid paternity or partner leave**.

- Leave may be taken continuously or in up to three blocks.
- It must normally be used within 90 calendar days of the birth.
- At least five working days should be taken during the first 30 calendar days unless the employee requests another arrangement.
- The employee should provide reasonable supporting documentation to People & HR.

## 5. Adoption and Placement Leave

For the adoption or formal placement of a child:

- One primary caregiver receives **90 calendar days of paid adoption leave**.
- A second caregiver receives **15 working days of paid partner leave**.
- Caregiver designation is confirmed through People & HR and may not be changed after leave begins except for a material family event.
- Leave may begin on the placement date or up to five working days earlier when required for official procedures.

This section applies to legally recognized adoption or placement arrangements. People & HR will confidentially assess other permanent-care arrangements.

## 6. Pregnancy Loss and Neonatal Loss

An employee experiencing pregnancy loss may use up to **10 working days of paid compassionate leave**, in addition to any medically certified sick leave or greater statutory entitlement.

Where a child dies after birth, the employee is entitled to the bereavement leave described in Section 9 and any additional statutory protection. People & HR will coordinate the applicable leave sensitively and confidentially.

## 7. Return to Work

At least 15 calendar days before the expected return, the employee and manager should confirm:

- Return date.
- Current role priorities.
- Required access restoration.
- On-call or customer-coverage expectations.
- Any approved phased return or flexible schedule.
- A private location and reasonable time for nursing or expressing milk, where required.

A phased return of up to four weeks may be approved by the manager and People & HR. The arrangement must state working hours, duration, pay treatment, and review date.

The employee returns to the same role or a reasonably equivalent role, subject to applicable law and legitimate organizational changes unrelated to the leave.

## 8. Keeping-in-Touch Arrangements

During extended parental leave, the employee may voluntarily agree to up to five keeping-in-touch sessions. Examples include:

- A reorganization briefing.
- Required security training.
- A product or customer update before return.
- A return-to-work planning discussion.

Participation is voluntary and must not become routine work. The manager and People & HR must agree in advance on timing and compensation treatment.

## 9. Bereavement Leave

Paid bereavement leave is available following the death of a family member.

| Relationship | Paid leave entitlement |
|---|---:|
| Spouse, recognized partner, child, stepchild, or parent | 10 working days |
| Sibling, grandparent, grandchild, parent-in-law, or legal guardian | 5 working days |
| Other close relative or person with a family-equivalent relationship | Up to 3 working days with manager and HR approval |

Leave may be taken continuously or, with approval, split around funeral, memorial, estate, or family responsibilities. Up to two additional working days may be approved when international or long-distance travel is required.

People & HR may request reasonable documentation but will avoid unnecessary intrusion. Employees may combine bereavement leave with annual leave under `05_Annual_and_Sick_Leave_Policy.pdf`.

## 10. Emergency Compassionate Leave

Employees may receive up to **five paid working days per calendar year** for an urgent and unforeseen situation involving a dependant or immediate family member, including:

- A serious accident or medical emergency.
- An unexpected breakdown in essential care arrangements.
- A domestic emergency that makes the employee's home temporarily unsafe.
- An urgent legal or safety matter involving an immediate family member.

The employee should notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible. Planned caregiving, routine appointments, and school holidays are not normally covered and should use annual leave or an agreed flexible schedule.

## 11. Unpaid Personal Leave

After available paid leave has been considered, an employee may request unpaid personal leave for a significant family or personal need.

- Requests up to 10 working days require manager and People & HR approval.
- Longer requests require department-leader, People & HR, and Finance review.
- Approval depends on operational coverage, prior leave, duration, employee circumstances, and legal obligations.
- The approval notice must explain benefits, payroll, accrual, equipment, and return-to-work treatment.

## 12. Sabbatical Leave

Employees with **three years of continuous service** may apply for an unpaid sabbatical of **four to twelve consecutive weeks**.

### 12.1 Eligibility

The employee must:

- Have completed three years of continuous service by the proposed start date.
- Have a current performance rating of Meets Expectations or higher.
- Have no active formal disciplinary process or performance improvement plan.
- Have completed any prior sabbatical at least three years earlier.
- Submit the request at least 90 calendar days in advance.

Eligibility does not guarantee approval.

### 12.2 Review Criteria

Approval considers:

- Team and leadership coverage.
- Customer commitments and release calendar.
- Availability of a temporary owner for critical responsibilities.
- Knowledge-transfer effort.
- Security and access implications.
- Timing of other extended leave.

Requests are reviewed by the manager, department leader, People & HR, and Finance. L5-L6 requests also require Executive Leadership Team approval.

### 12.3 Sabbatical Conditions

- Sabbaticals are unpaid unless a written exception is approved.
- Medical-insurance continuation and employee contributions are confirmed in the approval letter.
- Annual leave does not normally accrue during a full unpaid sabbatical month unless required by law.
- Company access may be suspended under `17_Information_Security_and_Data_Privacy.pdf`.
- The employee may not perform paid work for a competitor, customer, or supplier without written conflict-of-interest approval.
- Return is to the same role or a reasonably equivalent role, subject to legitimate organizational change.

## 13. Request and Approval Workflow

1. Employee contacts People & HR through the HR service portal.
2. People & HR confirms the leave category, entitlement, and required documentation.
3. The manager prepares a coverage and handover plan without collecting confidential medical details.
4. Required approvers review the request.
5. People & HR issues a written decision and records the leave in the HR system.
6. The employee updates the work calendar and completes the handover.
7. IT access changes are submitted when extended leave requires them.
8. The manager and employee complete a return-to-work discussion.

## 14. Handover Standard

For leave longer than 10 working days, the handover should identify:

- Active projects and status.
- Required decisions and deadlines.
- Named temporary owners.
- Customer, vendor, or on-call commitments.
- Links to current Jira items and approved documentation.
- Escalation contacts.

The handover must not require the employee to remain available during leave.

## 15. Confidentiality and Non-Retaliation

Medical and family documents are restricted to People & HR and authorized reviewers. Managers receive only the dates, work restrictions, and operational information they need.

Retaliation for requesting or taking approved leave is prohibited under `03_Code_of_Conduct_and_Ethics.txt` and may be reported through the confidential ethics channel.
