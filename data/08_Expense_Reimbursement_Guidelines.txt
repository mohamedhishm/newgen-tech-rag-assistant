EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT GUIDELINES

Owner: Finance and Accounting
Control partners: Procurement, People & HR, Information Security
Effective date: 1 January 2026
Version: 2.0
Classification: Internal

======================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
======================================================================

These guidelines define which business expenses NewGen Tech reimburses,
who must approve them, what evidence is required, and when claims must
be submitted.

They apply to employees and to contractors whose signed agreements allow
reimbursement. Expenses must be necessary, reasonable, connected to an
approved business purpose, and supported by an itemized receipt.

======================================================================
2. CORE RULES
======================================================================

- Obtain approval before making a material commitment.
- Use an approved company payment method when one is available.
- Do not divide one purchase into smaller claims to avoid an approval
  threshold.
- Submit claims within 30 calendar days of the transaction or trip end.
- Convert foreign currency using the rate recorded by the expense system
  on the transaction date. A card statement may be used when it shows the
  actual settled amount.
- Tips are reimbursable where customary, up to 15 percent unless a local
  service charge is already included.
- Personal loyalty points may be retained, but travel choices must not be
  made primarily to earn them.
- Reimbursement is normally paid with the next available payroll or
  supplier-payment cycle after approval.

Claims older than 60 calendar days require Finance Director approval.
Claims older than 90 calendar days are rejected unless a documented
exception, such as hospitalization or system failure, prevented timely
submission.

4. BUSINESS TRAVEL
======================================================================

Travel must be approved before booking. The request must state the
business purpose, destination, dates, expected cost, customer or event,
and project or department cost center.

4.1 Air and Rail

- Economy class is required for flights under six hours.
- Premium economy may be approved for a scheduled flight of six hours or
  longer.
- Business class requires Executive Leadership Team approval and a
  documented medical, accessibility, or critical business reason.
- Reasonable seat selection and one checked bag are reimbursable.
- Change fees are reimbursable when caused by business requirements or a
  documented emergency.
- Lounge memberships, first-class rail, and personal trip extensions are
  not reimbursable unless the personal extension reduces total company
  cost and is approved in advance.

4.2 Lodging

Employees should use safe, business-appropriate lodging near the work
location. Guideline caps per night, excluding taxes, are:

- Cairo or Alexandria: EGP 4,000
- Other Egypt locations: EGP 3,000
- Middle East and North Africa outside Egypt: USD 180 equivalent
- Europe: EUR 220 equivalent
- Other destinations: USD 200 equivalent

A higher rate requires pre-approval when event dates, safety, or local
availability make the cap impractical. Minibar, in-room entertainment,
spa services, and damage charges are personal expenses.

4.3 Ground Transportation

Reimbursable options include public transport, standard taxi or ride-hail,
airport transfer, and approved rental car. Employees should choose the
safest practical option rather than the lowest cost regardless of safety.
Luxury vehicles are not reimbursable. Rental-car fuel and tolls require
receipts. Traffic fines and parking violations are personal expenses.

4.4 Daily Meal and Incidental Per Diem

For approved overnight travel, the maximum daily allowance is:

- Egypt: EGP 1,000 per full travel day
- Middle East and North Africa outside Egypt: USD 50 equivalent
- Europe: EUR 65 equivalent
- Other destinations: USD 60 equivalent

On the first and last travel day, 75 percent of the daily cap applies.
Where breakfast, lunch, or dinner is provided by a hotel, conference, or
customer, reduce the cap by 20 percent, 30 percent, or 50 percent
respectively.

Alcohol is not reimbursable. Itemized receipts are required when the
expense system uses actual-cost reimbursement. Finance may publish a
country-specific statutory rate where required.

======================================================================
5. CUSTOMER AND TEAM MEALS
======================================================================

A customer meal claim must identify attendees, organizations, business
purpose, date, and location. The most senior NewGen Tech employee present
submits the claim.

Team meals require manager approval and a clear business purpose such as
an approved planning session, onboarding event, or recognition event.
Routine lunches for employees working at a company hub are not reimbursed.

======================================================================
6. HOME-OFFICE EQUIPMENT ALLOWANCE
======================================================================

A new regular employee in a remote-eligible role may claim up to
EGP 12,000 during the first 90 calendar days of employment for approved
home-office equipment.

Eligible items:

- Ergonomic chair
- Desk
- External monitor
- Keyboard and mouse
- Laptop stand or docking station
- Headset or webcam
- Surge protector or small uninterruptible power supply

Ineligible items:

- Home decoration
- General household furniture
- Television or gaming equipment
- Personal mobile phones
- Construction, rent, or utilities
- Equipment already supplied by NewGen Tech

Manager and IT approval are required before purchase of monitors,
docking stations, networking devices, or peripherals requiring technical
compatibility. Security-sensitive devices must follow
17_Information_Security_and_Data_Privacy.pdf.

Equipment reimbursed at 75 percent or more of its purchase price is a
NewGen Tech asset and must be recorded in the asset register. Finance and
IT determine return or residual-value treatment during offboarding under
09_Offboarding_and_Exit_Procedures.md.

======================================================================
7. COWORKING COSTS
======================================================================

Eligible remote employees may claim up to EGP 1,500 per calendar month
for coworking access under 02_Work_Hours_and_Hybrid_Policy.pdf.

Use expense category: Remote Work - Coworking.

The claim must include an itemized receipt and, for recurring membership,
prior manager approval. Unused amounts do not roll over. Food, commuting,
private clubs, hotel stays, and guest passes are excluded.

======================================================================
8. INTERNET, MOBILE, AND CONNECTIVITY
======================================================================

Employees in roles requiring regular customer calls, on-call coverage, or
remote work may claim up to EGP 600 per month for incremental business
internet or mobile-data cost with manager approval.

The claim must show the service period and amount. NewGen Tech does not
reimburse the full household bill where business use does not create an
incremental cost. Emergency data used during an incident may be reimbursed
outside the cap with the incident number and manager approval.

======================================================================
9. LEARNING, CERTIFICATIONS, AND CONFERENCES
======================================================================

Learning and conference claims count against the annual USD 1,200
employee budget defined in
07_Compensation_Benefits_and_Health_Insurance.pdf.

Pre-approval is required. Claims must include the course, certification,
or event name, provider, date, cost, and relationship to the employee's
development plan. Travel costs are charged separately to the department
travel budget only when specifically approved.

======================================================================
10. SOFTWARE, CLOUD SERVICES, AND SUBSCRIPTIONS
======================================================================

Employees must not purchase software, SaaS, cloud credits, data sets, AI
tools, or browser extensions with company data access through a personal
card unless Procurement has approved the purchase.

All such purchases follow 18_Procurement_and_Vendor_Approval_Workflow.txt,
including Information Security and Legal review where required. Automatic
renewal must be recorded before purchase.

======================================================================
11. REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION
======================================================================

Each claim must include:

- Transaction date
- Merchant or provider
- Itemized receipt or invoice
- Business purpose
- Cost center or project code
- Currency and amount
- Required approver
- Attendees for meals or events
- Travel request or procurement reference where applicable

A card slip without item detail is not sufficient. If a receipt is lost,
submit a Lost Receipt Declaration. More than two lost-receipt declarations
in a rolling 12-month period require Finance Controller review.

======================================================================
12. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
======================================================================

NewGen Tech does not reimburse:

- Personal purchases or commuting to the employee's normal work location
- Alcohol, tobacco, or recreational substances
- Fines, penalties, or late fees caused by the employee
- Personal insurance
- Childcare or pet care, unless an approved event policy states otherwise
- Cash withdrawals and unexplained foreign-exchange fees
- Gifts that violate 03_Code_of_Conduct_and_Ethics.txt
- Political donations
- Unapproved software or cloud services
- Expenses already paid by a customer, partner, or another employee
- Upgrades chosen for personal convenience

======================================================================
13. SUBMISSION AND REVIEW WORKFLOW
======================================================================

1. Employee creates the claim in the expense system.
2. Employee attaches evidence and selects the correct cost center.
3. Manager verifies business purpose and reasonableness.
4. Budget owner or specialist approver reviews when required.
5. Finance checks tax, receipt, policy, and duplicate-payment controls.
6. Approved reimbursement enters the next payment cycle.
7. Rejected claims are returned with a reason and correction instructions.

Employees must not approve their own expense. Delegated approvers must not
approve expenses that create a personal conflict of interest.

======================================================================
14. AUDIT, MISUSE, AND RECOVERY
======================================================================

Finance may audit claims before or after payment. NewGen Tech may recover
duplicate, unsupported, or ineligible payments through an agreed repayment
or lawful payroll deduction. Deliberately false claims are reviewed under
03_Code_of_Conduct_and_Ethics.txt.

Questions: submit an Expense Support request through the Finance portal.
