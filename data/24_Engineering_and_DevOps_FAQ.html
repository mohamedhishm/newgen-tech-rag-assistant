Engineering and DevOps FAQ

Document ID: NGT-FAQ-024 | NewGen Tech | Internal | Effective 1 January 2026

Which CI/CD system is authoritative?

GitHub pull requests and the approved pipeline are authoritative. Production artifacts must be built once, signed, and promoted rather than rebuilt.

What checks must pass before merge?

Unit tests, required integration tests, peer review, secret scanning, dependency scanning, SAST, and protected-branch rules for the repository.

Can I deploy directly from my laptop?

No. Production changes use the approved pipeline. Break-glass deployment requires an incident or change record and retrospective review.

What is staging for?

Staging is production-like validation for release candidates, migrations, performance tests, and acceptance. Use synthetic or approved masked data.

Can production data be copied into staging?

Not by default. Use synthetic data or an approved masked subset with explicit Security and data-owner approval.

How do I request database access?

Submit least-privilege access through the service portal with environment, database, role, business reason, duration, and manager or service-owner approval.

Is production database access permanent?

Normally no. Use time-limited access where supported, MFA, a controlled network path, and audited queries.

When are production deployment windows?

Standard windows are Sunday-Wednesday, 10 AM-2 PM EET. Low-risk configuration may run Sunday-Thursday, 10 AM-4 PM EET.

How do I handle a hotfix?

Open or link the incident/change record, identify risk and rollback, obtain the required approval, deploy through the pipeline, and monitor according to the runbook.

Where are secrets stored?

Only in the approved secrets manager. Do not place secrets in GitHub, Jira, Confluence, Slack, local .env files committed to source, or CI plain text.

How does on-call handover work?

PagerDuty rotation changes Sunday at 10 AM EET. Review open incidents, muted alerts, risky changes, certificates, capacity, and maintenance.

What qualifies as SEV-1?

Broad outage, confirmed cross-tenant exposure, active critical compromise, or unrecoverable data-loss threat.

How do feature flags work?

Flags separate deployment from activation. They need an owner, target tenants, removal date, safe default, and rollback behavior.

Can I create a new cloud service?

First complete architecture, security, cost, data-classification, ownership, observability, and procurement reviews as applicable.

Where do architecture decisions go?

Record material decisions in the approved Architecture Decision Record repository and link the relevant Jira epic and service catalog entry.
